Privacy Policy

How Yaara collects, stores, and protects your information across our mobile app and website.

In Summary: Yaara is an offline-first shared ledger app developed by Webcraftio. Your personal financial records, expense logs, and group balances are stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database. We do not sell your personal data, we do not run third-party advertising networks, and we do not track your financial transactions.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. Yaara only collects the data strictly necessary to provide bill-splitting, group ledgers, and optional cross-device synchronization.

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Details: When you choose to connect with friends via cloud sync, you may provide an email address or display name. Using Yaara completely offline requires no account creation whatsoever.
  • Ledger & Financial Records: Expense titles (e.g., "Supermarket", "Internet Bill"), monetary amounts, dates, split proportions, and notes entered into your groups.
  • Receipt Photos: Photos or camera captures of paper receipts that you voluntarily attach to an expense for bookkeeping proof.

B. Device Permissions & Hardware Access

To deliver core features, the Yaara Android application requests specific permissions. We only access these APIs upon direct user action:

Permission Technical Name Exact Purpose in Yaara
Camera android.permission.CAMERA Allows you to capture physical paper receipts to attach proof directly to an expense.
Photo Library expo-image-picker Allows you to select an existing invoice, screenshot, or receipt from your gallery.
Microphone / Audio android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO Used solely when you tap the assisted voice-logging tool to dictate an expense into draft.
Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS Delivers alerts when a flatmate logs an expense, records a repayment, or accepts an invite.

2. Offline-First Architecture & Data Storage

Unlike traditional cloud-only banking applications, Yaara is built with an offline-first local repository:

  • Local SQLite Storage: All expenses, repayments, and groups are written instantly to your device's private storage. If you lose internet access, your ledger functions at 100% capacity.
  • End-to-End Cloud Sync (Supabase): When connected, groups that have cloud sync enabled synchronize via encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections to our secure backend database powered by Supabase.
  • Zero Bank Integration: Yaara does not connect to your bank accounts, credit cards, or digital wallets. Yaara is a social bookkeeping ledger, not a payment gateway. No monetary funds pass through Yaara.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Calculate mathematically accurate group balances, proportional shares, and debt reconciliations.
  • Synchronize shared balances between verified group members who enter your invite link or code.
  • Process receipt images locally or through secure vision models strictly to extract item subtotals, taxes, and service fees into your expense draft.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Advertising

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal or financial data.

We do not use Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, or any behavioral ad trackers. The infrastructure providers we rely on strictly process operational data under strict confidentiality:

  • Supabase: Encrypted cloud database and authentication infrastructure.
  • Expo / EAS: Mobile application updates and crash telemetry for stability.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full sovereignty over your information:

  • Local Data: Uninstalling the Yaara app or clearing app storage immediately deletes all local SQLite databases and saved receipt images from your phone.
  • Cloud Account & Group Deletion: You can delete any group, archive past records, or request complete account deletion directly from the Settings > Account screen in the app. Upon deletion, all associated ledgers, history, and uploaded receipts are permanently purged from our servers within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Yaara is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.

7. Compliance with Google Play Developer Policies

This privacy policy adheres strictly to Google Play's User Data policies, including requirements for Prominent Disclosure, Data Safety questionnaires, and runtime permission transparency.
