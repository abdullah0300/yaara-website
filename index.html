Is Yaara just for splitting restaurant bills?

No. You can track hostel costs, road trips, chai runs, daily food delivery, personal loans, and repayments. A dinner bill is just one of many moments it handles effortlessly.

Does Yaara send or collect money?

No. Pay your friends through whatever you already use (cash, bank transfer, SadaPay, Nayapay, Easypaisa), and record it in Yaara. Yaara is a clean shared ledger, so there are no bank logins or transaction fees.

Does everyone have to join?

No. You can record expenses for friends even if they haven't joined yet. When they do install Yaara, you can share the group so everyone sees live balances.

Can we choose an unequal split?

Yes. Equal, exact amounts, percentage, and item-based splits let you match reality. If someone only had tea while others had dinner, everyone pays only what they owe.

What happens when I’m offline?

Yaara works completely offline. Add expenses on road trips or remote spots, and everything syncs seamlessly once you reconnect.
