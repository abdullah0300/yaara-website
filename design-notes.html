Good company.
Clear money.

Yaara is a shared-money record for expenses, informal personal loans and repayments. The story starts with the people who share life, makes a familiar money moment concrete, and lets visitors understand the numbers for themselves.

Product basis: the supplied Yaara research pack dated 23 October 2026 and the supplied cobalt/lime branding pack. The report is the product source for this concept; the actual repository and production app screens were not supplied. All displayed records are invented examples. The interactive expense walkthrough uses illustrative records, not a live account.

Content and release boundaries

  • Public download URLs, pricing and release dates are not invented. The primary action lets visitors explore a working split example while availability remains a pilot.
  • There are no fabricated testimonials, user totals, awards for Yaara or guarantees of repayment.
  • Repayments happen outside Yaara. The UI records them.
  • Separate currency records do not imply exchange-rate conversion.
  • Recurring drafts require review before posting. They are not automatic deductions.
  • Motion respects the system preference, with a visible off switch. Native scrolling, semantic anchors and keyboard-accessible tabs remain usable.
