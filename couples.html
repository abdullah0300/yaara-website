What shared costs can couples record in Yaara?

Record purchases you have both agreed to share, such as groceries, date nights, transport, household supplies or holiday costs. Each expense keeps its payer and agreed shares. Personal purchases can stay outside shared costs; money lent for something personal can be recorded as a separate loan.

How can we split groceries that include personal items?

Look at the receipt together and agree which items are shared and which are personal. Record the agreed shared cost and use equal, exact or item-based shares as appropriate. Keep the receipt with the expense so the amount is easier to understand later.

Can we use different shares for different bills?

Yes. Your agreement can vary by expense. You might split one purchase equally and record exact amounts or percentages for another. Yaara records the shares you agree; it does not calculate an income-based recommendation or decide what is fair for you.

What if we take turns paying for different things?

Record each shared purchase with the person who paid and both agreed shares. Taking turns does not necessarily mean equal amounts were covered. Keeping the expenses and any repayments recorded gives you the information to review what remains.

How can couples track shared expenses?

Agree which costs you share and how to divide each one. In Yaara, record the amount, who paid, each person’s agreed share and any repayments. This keeps the details together across purchases paid by either person.

Do we have to split every bill equally?

No. Yaara supports equal, exact, percentage and item-based shares. Decide together what fits the expense, then record those shares. The app records your agreement; it does not decide what is fair for your relationship.

Can we keep personal spending separate?

Yes. Only add purchases you have agreed to share as shared expenses. If you lend your partner money for something personal, record that separately as a personal loan.

Can we make a group for a holiday or moving home?

Yes. Use a Yaara group for a particular plan or your ongoing shared costs. Record each purchase with its payer, participants and agreed shares. Different expenses can have different shares.

What if one of us only pays part of the amount back?

Record a partial repayment. For example, if PKR 2,000 is due and PKR 800 is returned, PKR 1,200 remains. The payment happens outside Yaara; record it in the app afterwards.

Does Yaara need a joint bank account?

Yaara keeps records of shared costs, money lent and repayments. Pay each other using your usual payment method. Yaara does not move money between accounts.

Do both people need connected accounts?

You can keep personal records for a contact without a connected account. For your partner to access shared records, they need the appropriate connection or group access.
