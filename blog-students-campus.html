The 30-Second Solution

Campus Rule: Student budgets have zero margin for error. When team members buy project hardware, poster boards, or canteen lunch platters, photograph the receipt immediately. A shared group ledger with zero subscription paywalls ensures everyone reimburses their exact share without awkward reminders.

University life is full of spontaneous micro-expenses: a shared ride to campus, photocopy bundles for midterm notes, samosas and chai between lectures, and last-minute hardware parts for the semester project.

Because each transaction is small (PKR 300 here, PKR 850 there), students often hesitate to ask for their money back. By the end of the semester, one diligent student has quietly subsidized thousands of rupees out of their personal allowance.

Higher Education Study: Over 68% of university students admit that unpaid small debts for group assignments and canteen orders create lingering awkwardness between friends and classmates.
