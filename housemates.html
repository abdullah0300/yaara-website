How can roommates keep track of shared expenses?

Keep one household record with the amount, who paid, who shares each expense and their agreed shares. Record repayments when they happen. Yaara brings these details together in a household group so the record doesn’t depend on one person’s memory.

Which household expenses can we record in Yaara?

Record the costs you agree to share, such as rent, utilities, internet, groceries and household supplies. Choose the people involved in each expense. Keep personal purchases separate unless the people sharing them have agreed otherwise.

Can roommates split rent or groceries unequally?

Yes. In Yaara you can record equal, exact, percentage, weighted or item-based shares. Agree on the split together, then choose the method that matches that expense. Your rent agreement can differ from your grocery agreement.

What if a roommate is away for part of the month?

Agree together which costs they still share and which they don’t. In Yaara, choose participants and agreed shares for each expense. The record follows the details you enter; it doesn’t decide an absence policy or automatically prorate bills.

Are recurring household bills posted automatically?

No. Recurring bill drafts can be reused, but you review the amount, people and shares before posting each expense. A familiar bill still gets a fresh check.

Can we record a partial repayment?

Yes. Record the amount returned and see what remains. For example, if a housemate owes PKR 2,000 and returns PKR 500, the remaining amount is PKR 1,500.

Does Yaara pay rent or collect money from roommates?

No. Yaara keeps records of shared expenses and repayments. Pay each other through the method you normally use, then record the repayment in Yaara.
