How can a group track shared travel expenses in Yaara?

Create a Yaara group for the trip. Add each shared expense with the amount, payer, currency, participants and agreed shares. Keep useful receipts with their expenses. Review the group after the trip and record repayments made through your usual payment method.

Which trip costs can we record?

Record costs the group agrees to share, such as accommodation, fuel, taxis, meals, groceries and activities. Each expense can have different participants and shares, so personal purchases and optional plans don’t have to be divided among everyone.

What if someone skips an activity or joins the trip later?

Choose the participants for each expense in Yaara. Include only the friends who agreed to share that particular cost. A person can remain part of the trip group while being excluded from a ride, meal or activity they did not share.

How do we split a restaurant bill when orders are different?

Agree which items and extras are shared, then record equal, exact, percentage or item-based shares in Yaara. Use the arrangement your friends agreed for that meal instead of assuming every restaurant bill needs an equal split.

What if different friends pay for the stay, fuel and meals?

Record each expense with its own payer and agreed shares in the same trip group. Paying for different things does not necessarily mean everyone covered the same amount. Keeping those details and repayments recorded makes the final review easier.

Can I add an expense without a signal?

Yes. You can add records offline in Yaara. Queued changes sync when you reconnect; your friends will not receive those updates while you remain offline. Reconnect before reviewing the trip together.

Does Yaara convert different currencies into one trip total?

No. Yaara keeps expenses and balances separate by currency. It does not automatically convert them into one total. Check the currency of each record and agree any currency exchange outside the app.

Can a friend repay only part of what they owe?

Yes. Record each repayment in Yaara after the money is returned, including part payments. For example, PKR 3,700 due minus PKR 1,000 returned leaves PKR 2,700 still to receive.

Does Yaara transfer money between travellers?

No. Yaara records shared expenses and repayments. Pay each other through the method you normally use, then record the payment in the app.
